Word formation

In Part 3 you read a text with eight gaps and change the word in capitals so it fits each gap. You'll often need a prefix, a suffix, a plural or a negative form. These exercises use the word formation list from Ready for First.

Use British spelling, as in the book: organiser, behaviour, colourful, skilful.

Before you write, ask yourself: what kind of word goes here (noun, adjective, verb, adverb)? Is it positive or negative? Singular or plural? Then check your spelling, because a misspelt word scores zero.

-ing or -ed adjectives?

-ed adjectives describe how we feel: I'm tired after the journey.
-ing adjectives describe the thing or person that causes the feeling: The journey was very tiring.
